## Predicting Vehicle CO2 Emissions: A Multicollinearity Case Study with OLS and Ridge Regression

A vehicle emits carbon dioxide (CO₂) whenever its internal combustion engine (ICE) is running and burning fuel. It damages the environment by trapping heat in the atmosphere, driving global climate change.



## QUESTIONS
1. Determine or test the influence of different variables on the emission of CO2.
2. What are the most influencing features that affect the CO2 emission the most?
3. Will there be any difference in the CO2 emissions when Fuel Consumption for City and Highway are considered separately and when their weighted variable interaction is considered?

Predict CO2 emissions from engine size, cylinders, and fuel consumption (city, highway, combined).
